# Two-minute pitch narration

The visuals use the local synthetic demo. Azure deployment, Foundry generation, and Speech output are described as configured capabilities, not shown as live cloud results.

1. **Football moves fast.** “Football moves fast. The story behind a moment can disappear before anyone explains it. Broadcast teams need useful context while the match is still moving.”
2. **The problem.** “An analyst sees a pass, a press, or a shot. The question is why it matters. Producers need a clear cue, and fans need a version they can follow.”
3. **The studio.** “PitchPulse AI turns structured match events into an analyst view. This is the real local dashboard, driven by our synthetic match stream. Events and positions update as the feed arrives.”
4. **The insight.** “The engine calculates pass difficulty, nearby defensive pressure, passing accuracy, and a location-based expected-goals estimate. These are transparent analytical signals, not predictions of the final result.”
5. **The pipeline.** “Events are validated, sent through Azure Event Hubs, and processed by a coordinated agent crew. Two WebSocket streams deliver the same factual moment to studio and fan screens.”
6. **The fan feed.** “Here is the fan experience in Spanish. A viewer can choose one of five languages and three styles. Factual updates still work when a language model is not configured.”
7. **Optional AI and voice.** “When Microsoft Foundry and Azure Speech are configured, the feed can add personalized commentary and spoken updates. Those slower tasks follow the factual event, so the immediate feed stays responsive.”
8. **Verification.** “We test the Event Hubs path with the official emulator, build both containers in CI, and benchmark local fan-out. The final Azure staging check measures real delivery to both screens.”
9. **Close.** “PitchPulse AI helps analysts explain the play and fans follow it their way. The next step is a licensed data feed and broadcaster pilot. Every match tells a story. PitchPulse AI brings it to life.”
